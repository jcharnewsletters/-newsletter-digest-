Tokenized Stocks Concentration Risk

Mezzanine Risk-Tranched DeFi Yields

Unreleased Anthropic Model Proves 18-Year-Old Quantum Computing Conjecture

  • Physical Superintelligence (PSI) used an unreleased Anthropic AI model to prove the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture, unsolved since 2008, which concerns limits on quantum computers' advantage on 'messy' problems where no single data point dominates.
  • The proof was formally verified using Lean (a logic-checking tool that validates every step of a mathematical proof, preventing confident-sounding but incorrect reasoning), and PSI also published the conjecture's statement in Lean for public audit.
  • Four Stanford researchers who had solved a smaller version of the problem reviewed and praised the proof as a major result, though the wider mathematical community (including the conjecture's original namesakes) has not yet weighed in.
