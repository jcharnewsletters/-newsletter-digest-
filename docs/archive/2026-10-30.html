Binance Hacked for $6.3 Billion in Largest Crypto Heist Ever

  • Hackers stole $6.3 billion from Binance, the largest cryptocurrency exchange heist in history.
  • Attackers reportedly gained access via a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop and executed authorized transfers across 10 different blockchains.
  • Blockchain intelligence firm TRM Labs attributes the attack to TraderTraitor, North Korea's elite hacking unit, with 'high confidence'.
  • Binance froze roughly $3 billion of the stolen funds on-chain and suspended withdrawals while conducting a security overhaul.
  • Binance pledged to cover all user losses from operational reserves, future earnings, and emergency funds, asserting 'user funds are safe'.
  • Experts say most stolen tokens are unlikely to be recovered, though frozen stablecoins (cryptocurrencies pegged to stable assets like the US dollar) could be clawed back if issuers cooperate.
  • Bitcoin fell as much as 5% to around $77,000 (its lowest since September) before paring losses to under 2%; Ethereum and Ripple also sold off.
  • Industry fears the hack could trigger a broader confidence crisis in crypto similar to the FTX and Mt. Gox collapses, though some analysts believe the impact could be short-lived if Binance restores operations smoothly.
  • Regulatory scrutiny is expected to intensify, potentially affecting crypto market access and custody standards, especially after the midterm elections.
