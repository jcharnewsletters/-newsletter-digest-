Tokenized U.S. Stocks Moving to Blockchain Rails

  • OKXICE, a joint venture between crypto exchange OKX and NYSE parent ICE, filed with the SEC to launch 24/7 blockchain-based trading of 60+ U.S. stocks including Tesla, Apple, Nvidia, and other Magnificent 7 names.
  • Tokenized shares retain dividends and voting rights and trade via liquidity pools (automated systems matching buyers and sellers) on blockchain networks.
  • SEC's new conditional 'innovation exemption' gives trading venues a 5-year window to experiment with blockchain trading, bypassing traditional exchange definitions.
  • Potential benefits include instant settlement (vs. current 2-day clearing) and 24/7 global access, but risks include third-party token issuance difficult to audit, market fragmentation, and dependency on stablecoins (dollar-backed cryptocurrencies like USDC, USDG, USDT).
  • Coinbase Vice Chair called tokenization 'the biggest upgrade to financial infrastructure since electronic trading.'
