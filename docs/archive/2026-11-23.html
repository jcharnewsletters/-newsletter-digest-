Anthropic's IPO disappoints despite strong debut

COP31 climate talks collapse without agreement

  • The COP31 summit in Antalya, Turkey ended Sunday after a two-week marathon session with no consensus on fossil fuel reduction, carbon capture (technology that captures CO2 emissions before they reach the atmosphere), or climate financing for developing nations.
  • The EU pushed for an explicit fossil fuel phase-out while Russia argued rapid supply cuts would cause shortages, price shocks, and energy poverty — a split that proved unresolvable.
  • Market reaction underscored the divide: fossil fuel stocks surged (XLE energy ETF +35% in 2026, ExxonMobil +33% YTD) while clean energy underperformed (ICLN clean energy ETF -15%, NextEra Energy +4%).
  • EU climate chief Wopke Hoekstra said the failure shows multilateralism (countries working together through international bodies like the UN) is in 'dire straits' and that alternatives outside the UN process are needed.

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) Noted the irony that host nation Turkey is itself aggressively expanding fossil fuel exploration and trade, and that lack of U.S. participation stalled financing talks for developing nations.

Bloomberg Talks were specifically aimed at crafting a strategy for the near-certainty that global temperatures will exceed the 1.5°C threshold set in the Paris Agreement (the international goal to limit warming to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels).
