Trump considers/backs US diesel export ban amid record prices

  • Diesel prices hit a record $6.53/gallon Tuesday, up 77% year-over-year, with California ($8.44/gal) and Washington ($7.47/gal) highest.
  • Trump voiced support for banning diesel exports; Treasury Secretary Bessent said the idea is under feasibility study.
  • US supplies about 1.5 million of the 8 million barrels traded globally each day (roughly 20% of global diesel trade); cutting exports could force refiners to reduce output across all fuel types, potentially raising rather than lowering US prices long-term.
  • Mexico and Latin America depend heavily on US diesel for agricultural transport; low Mexican inventories could cause physical fuel shortages within days, risking food-export disruption and higher US grocery prices.
  • Europe, already in a fuel crisis, could lose the equivalent of 1/7 of EU diesel demand (500,000 barrels/day) if US exports are cut, forcing buyers to scramble for alternatives.

Meta's Muse AI agent gains traction

  • Muse, Meta's AI agent app, is topping app charts with strong user demand and praise from tech leaders ahead of Meta's Connect conference.
  • Unlike chatbots, Muse lets users create personalized AI agents (multitasking programs that can act on the internet on a user's behalf, e.g., browsing, purchasing, negotiating).
  • Meta is testing a human concierge service to support Muse users as AI inference demands (computing power needed to run AI models) grow.
  • Meta's built-in reach via Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp gives it a strong path to AI monetization; CEO Zuckerberg plans to keep Muse free/low-cost while taking a cut of commercial transactions.
  • Rapid AI agent capability (web navigation, purchasing, negotiating) raises concerns about job displacement in customer-service and transaction-heavy roles, though new AI-adjacent jobs may offset losses.
  • Intel shares jumped 25% in five days as investors bet on rising CPU (central processing unit, the chip that executes computing tasks) demand tied to AI agent adoption.
