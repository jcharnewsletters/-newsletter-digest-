Nvidia's Record $150B Stock Buyback

  • Nvidia authorized a $150 billion increase to its stock buyback program, the largest single expansion in corporate history, bringing total authorization to $235 billion through fiscal 2028.
  • The increase exceeds Apple's previous record $110 billion authorization (2024) and dwarfs full buyback programs ever run by Apple, Microsoft, Meta, or Alphabet.
  • Stock moved only 1.4% on the news, suggesting markets read it as confirmation of strong cash generation rather than a surprise; Nvidia's market cap sits at $5.5 trillion versus Apple's $4.9 trillion.
  • Despite being the core AI (artificial intelligence) play, Nvidia trades at a lower forward price-to-earnings multiple than the S&P 500 and all other 'Magnificent Seven' tech stocks, drawing comparisons to legacy energy stocks like ExxonMobil despite a much steeper growth trajectory.
