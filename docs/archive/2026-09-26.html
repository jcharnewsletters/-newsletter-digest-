10-Year Treasury Yield Hits Highest Level Since 2007–2008

  • 10-year Treasury yield (interest rate the government pays to borrow money for 10 years) rose to between 5.15% and 5.2%, the highest since 2007–2008.

  • Rising yields typically increase borrowing costs and reduce stock valuations, though major indices still posted modest-to-strong weekly gains (S&P 500 +1.21%, Nasdaq +2.06%, Dow +0.28%).
