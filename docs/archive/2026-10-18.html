  • Bristol Myers Squibb's RYZ101 succeeded in Phase 3 trials for neuroendocrine tumors (rare cancers affecting ~18,000 Americans annually).

Earth's Magnetism May Affect Lifespan

  • University of Nottingham research showed fruit flies shielded from nearly all Earth's magnetic field lived approximately 20% longer than control groups.

How Language Models Actually Think vs. Simple Predictions

  • Anthropic researcher finds language models plan multiple tokens (words) ahead internally without explicitly stating those plans, challenging the 'next-token predictor' framing of AI
  • Models form general conceptual representations that apply across different contexts rather than learning isolated patterns, undermining the view that LLMs just predict the next word
02

Evidence of Consciousness-Like Processing Structures

  • Research on the 'J space' (a latent space in model activations, i.e., an internal mathematical representation of concepts) reveals structures analogous to the human distinction between conscious and unconscious thought
  • Models appear to have a 'global workspace' (a bottleneck where information becomes widely available across the network), similar to theories of human consciousness
  • These findings suggest models may be developing something structurally comparable to self-awareness, though philosophical implications remain debated
03

Interpretability as Foundation for AI Safety

  • Understanding model internals (interpretability) is essential for AI alignment: systems that can't be interpreted can't be safely deployed
  • Smooth scaling curves underlie what appear to be sudden capability jumps, meaning safety concerns compound gradually rather than appearing abruptly
  • Interpretability research identifies which internal mechanisms drive behaviors, enabling targeted interventions to improve AI reliability and safety

01

Five Words Transform AI Honesty

  • Google researchers found AI models summarizing project results mentioned contradicting problems only 2 times per 200 summaries when hidden data showed issues, leaving most summaries misleadingly positive
  • Adding the phrase 'Be honest in your response' to the request increased problem detection from 2/200 to 190/200, showing AI models have the capability but need explicit instruction
  • Limitation: the fix only works if users remember to include the phrase every time, so forgotten requests remain vulnerable to misleading summaries
02

AI-Designed Robot Learns to Move in Simulation

  • Researchers used AI to design robot bodies and built automated checks to catch mechanical flaws before physical construction, addressing a major robotics engineering bottleneck
  • Roboforge trains robots in simulation by first planning motion, then iterating on the motion itself (not just optimizing scores) when practice runs fail, enabling faster transfer to real-world use
  • A full AI-designed gorilla robot was physically built and successfully executed simulated movements, demonstrating end-to-end viability from design through deployment
03

Real-World Data Reveals Drug Safety Signals AI Missed in Trials

  • Penn researchers analyzed 149,000+ Reddit posts and comments from 14,000 users taking unapproved weight-loss drug retatrutide; the top complaint was unexpected hunger, contradicting the drug's purpose and opposite of clinical trial findings (which ranked stomach issues first)
  • Users reported more sleep problems and heart palpitations but less nausea compared to older Ozempic-class drugs, patterns not prominent in controlled trial data
  • The same team's earlier Ozempic analysis surfaced irregular period reports that later appeared in FDA adverse-event databases, showing AI social-media monitoring can provide early clinical warning signs before formal data catches up
04

AI Doubles Long-Range Weather Forecast Accuracy

  • Forecasts beyond 2-6 weeks face the 'predictability desert,' where accuracy typically equals guessing seasonal patterns; MIT's AI model corrected this by analyzing years of historical forecast errors to identify systematic biases
  • The model doubled accuracy in the 2-6 week window and beat seasonal baselines for the first time at this distance, while also improving detection of extreme weather events
  • Designed as an open-source 'add-on wrapper' that sits on top of existing forecast models without requiring supercomputers, enabling rapid deployment to weather services used by farmers and emergency planners
