Dodo De-extinction Progress

  • Colossal Biosciences reports 95% of the dodo genome has been sequenced, with AI platforms narrowing focus to ~2,300 genetic targets linked to the bird's distinctive beak.
  • The species, extinct since the 1600s due to human settlement and introduced rats in Mauritius, could be reintroduced to its native habitat.
  • No hatch date has been announced, but the company maintains the project is on track.

Cancer Drug RYZ101 Shows Promise

  • Bristol Myers Squibb's RYZ101 succeeded in Phase 3 trials for neuroendocrine tumors (rare cancers affecting ~18,000 Americans annually).
  • The drug functions as a targeted therapy, latching onto tumor cells and delivering short-range radiation that damages their DNA.
  • Specific efficacy numbers have not yet been disclosed, but results offer hope to patients with limited medical alternatives.

Earth's Magnetism May Affect Lifespan

  • University of Nottingham research showed fruit flies shielded from nearly all Earth's magnetic field lived approximately 20% longer than control groups.
  • The mechanism remains unknown, and whether the effect applies to humans is still undetermined.
  • Findings could inform understanding of how long-term space travel beyond Earth's magnetic field may impact astronaut health and longevity.
