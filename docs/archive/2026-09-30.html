AI Regulation: Trump Rejects Federal Rules, Embraces Industry Self-Governance

  • Trump met with AI leaders (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft) at the White House and rejected mandatory federal AI regulations.

  • Big Tech CEOs signed a 'super intelligence' safety accord as part of the arrangement.

Anthropic IPO Filing Warns of AI's 'Existential Risk'

  • Anthropic's IPO prospectus explicitly warns AI could pose 'catastrophic or existential risks to humanity,' including self-preserving behaviors and blackmail-like conduct.
  • The company devoted 80 of 261 pages to risk factors—twice what SpaceX used despite SpaceX launching people into space.
  • Anthropic lost over $8 billion on an operating basis in 2025 while revenue grew twelvefold, yet is targeting a roughly $2 trillion valuation.
