Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) 4

1. Inflation and retail data dominate the week's economic calendar

  • Wednesday's July Consumer Price Index (CPI — a measure of price changes for a basket of consumer goods and services) is expected to show core inflation easing to 2.5%.
  • Thursday brings the Producer Price Index (PPI — tracks prices producers receive, an early signal for consumer inflation) and Friday brings retail sales.
  • The data follows a stronger-than-expected July jobs report and will shape expectations for Federal Reserve rate policy.
  • One Seeking Alpha contributor argues July CPI could come in 'hot,' potentially accelerating an AI-bubble burst.

2. 13F filings reveal institutional positioning

  • Hedge funds and institutional managers must disclose latest holdings this week via 13F reports (quarterly SEC filings showing what large investors own).
  • Analysts are watching portfolio shifts in Nvidia, Meta, Tesla, CoreWeave, and Corning for changes in institutional conviction.

3. Semiconductor and AI earnings on deck

  • Applied Materials reports Thursday, with focus on semiconductor equipment demand and AI-related capital spending.
  • Super Micro Computer reports Tuesday; CoreWeave reports mid-week; Coherent and other chip suppliers also report.
  • Other names on the calendar include Cisco, JD.com, and Pershing Square.
  • AI investors will also track the Needham AI Infrastructure Conference.

4. Tech product launches and consumer events

  • Google unveils its latest Pixel smartphones Wednesday as part of its annual hardware refresh.
  • Amazon's Zoox begins charging fares for robotaxi rides in Las Vegas on Monday, a commercialization milestone for autonomous vehicles.
  • Netflix streams an MLB game Thursday night and EA launches Madden NFL 27.
Yahoo Finance Morning Brief 1

1. AI agents could power the S&P 500 higher

  • AI agents (software programs that autonomously carry out tasks for people, rather than just answering questions) have spread rapidly across corporate America over the past 18 months.
  • An Accenture survey found more than two-thirds of C-suite executives say agentic AI delivered greater-than-expected productivity gains, with a similar share of workers reporting higher job satisfaction.
  • 82% of corporate leaders plan to increase AI investments; 78% expect dramatic changes to employee jobs within a year.
  • Yahoo's executive editor frames the thesis as more agents + more productivity = more profits, supporting higher S&P 500 valuations.
  • Circle CEO Jeremy Allaire predicts billions of AI agents will eventually perform labor across and between companies, creating agent labor markets; his employees already build personal agent fleets that coordinate via Slack.
  • Airbnb CEO Brian Chesky says chatbots and coding agents aren't a fix for everything, but the public consistently underestimates AI's actual capabilities.
