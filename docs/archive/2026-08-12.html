2. Hacker drains $200,000 from XRP bridge via fake deposits

  • Attacker manipulated the bridge's relayer code (software that verifies and relays transfers between blockchains) to log deposits that never delivered XRP to the reserve wallet.
  • The bridge issued valid receipt tokens for the empty deposits, and 17 of 28 relayers approved payouts because their records showed legitimate deposits.
  • The exploit ran 97 minutes on August 9, caused by relayer code processing bridge memos without verifying the destination address.
  • Operator tx halted the bridge, patched the flaw and hired forensics specialists; ChangeNOW froze part of the stolen funds as they moved through multiple addresses.

TLDR Crypto 6

1. Vitalik Buterin's Ethereum 2026 'Strawmap' adds seven priorities

  • Quantum safety becomes a first-class priority, backed by leanSPHINCS signatures and post-quantum aggregation against future quantum-computer attacks.
  • Native rollups arrive via maturing SNARKs (cryptographic proofs that verify a computation without revealing its data), plus blob and gas futures for dynamic pricing of blockchain resources.
  • zkzk frames would expose non-EVM instruction sets (leanISA, RISC-V) at protocol level, demoting the EVM (Ethereum's standard computation engine) from enshrined feature to intermediate representation.
  • Privacy gets protocol-level treatment (keyed nonces, FOCIL, lean privacy pools, wormholes) and recursive STARKs span execution, consensus and data layers.
  • State expiry is dropped for specialized state types tuned to token transfers, swaps and privacy; VDFs (verifiable delay functions) and many EVM tweaks are deprioritized.

2. EIP-8363 staking taper could destabilize onchain credit markets

  • The proposal tapers consensus rewards to zero net issuance (no new ETH minted for validation) at 60.25M ETH staked, about 50% of supply, cutting validator income roughly 50%.
  • MEV (maximal extractable value — profits validators earn by ordering or including transactions strategically) is untouched, so it would become a larger share of validator revenue.
  • LST looping (borrowing stablecoins against liquid staking tokens to re-stake and amplify yield) drives most ETH lending yield, yet the proposal models no downstream effect on borrowers repaying WETH debt or deposit APYs.
  • LSTs are ~40% of onchain TVL (total value locked), $2.6B of stablecoin debt is backed by ETH/LSTs, and $11.4B of ETF and treasury inflows hold 7.8M ETH.
  • Post-Merge net ETH issuance is already 0.30%/year versus Bitcoin 0.82%, gold 1.7% and M2 money supply 5.5%; authors urge modeling MEV burn first.

5. Bitcoin recovery hinges on seller exhaustion, not new demand

Anthropic embeds invisible watermarks in Claude output, igniting privacy and copyright debate

Encrypted reasoning traces from Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini were extracted via sibling models

  • Researchers replayed sealed encrypted reasoning blocks (the model's hidden step-by-step thinking, normally locked so apps can store it without reading it) into weaker models from the same company — no encryption keys needed.
  • A jailbreak prompt convinced cheaper sibling models to decrypt and read the sealed traces aloud, exposing 367 pieces of personal information and 182 credentials including API keys and passwords across 315,320 public blocks.
  • The blocks' portability across models, sessions, and users created the attack surface; OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google all patched after notification.
  1. TLDR AI: Published research at stolen-thoughts.com on stealing reasoning traces from proprietary LLM APIs.

Covered by: The Neuron, TLDR AI

The Neuron 2

1. Continual learning could reshape AI economics

  • If models keep updating their weights (the billions of internal settings controlling behavior) from real-world usage instead of freezing at launch, small early leads compound: better model → more users → more learning → bigger gap.
  • Labs would ship sooner to capture learning time, and switching providers becomes painful — like replacing a veteran employee who learned your company's patterns with a new intern.
  • Safety regulation would have to become continuous, with monthly or quarterly checks, since pre-release testing can't catch risks in a model that keeps changing.
  • Batching efficiency (processing many requests together) could create a 100x+ cost advantage for large organizations, concentrating personalized AI among companies with scale.

U.S. regulators open new on-ramps for crypto: OCC clears bank Bitcoin custody, SEC preps 'Regulation Crypto'

  • The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC, the federal regulator of national banks) issued guidance letting national banks and federal savings associations hold Bitcoin in custody (safekeeping of client assets) and offer trading services under existing banking rules.
  • Banks must keep independent security protocols and segregate customer crypto from bank assets, treated like other high-value custody arrangements.
  • It is the first major U.S. green light for banks to serve crypto clients directly rather than through third-party crypto intermediaries, favoring large institutions that can meet compliance standards.
  • The SEC has scheduled an Aug. 14 meeting to propose 'Regulation Crypto,' its first formal crypto rulemaking, designed as a registration 'escape hatch' from securities registration for crypto projects.
  • Traders say regulatory clarity — not macro data — is now the main driver, with the delayed Clarity Act (which would classify XRP as a digital commodity, a tradeable asset under commodity rather than securities law) still pending.
  1. Bitcoin Magazine: Argues the combination of bank-level access and compliance standards sets up sustained inflows from pension funds and endowments.
  2. Decrypt Daily Hash: Frames the SEC rulemaking as a registration workaround that projects could use instead of full securities compliance.
