Paramount Skydance-Warner Bros. merger clears final regulatory hurdle

  • California AG and 11 states settled lawsuits, clearing the way for Paramount Skydance's Warner Bros. acquisition, valued at $70B-$110B depending on source.
  • Paramount must maintain California production lots for five years and commit an additional $1.5B to U.S. film production.
  • Key concession: Paramount must release 30 films annually in theaters or pay $30 million per film shortfall, risking its Miramax stake.
  • California AG required independent editorial boards with unbiased journalists for CNN and CBS as part of the settlement.
  • Combined entity will own Paramount studios, CBS News, HBO, DC Studios, TNT, and Peacock, creating the largest media bundle and pressuring rivals Disney and Netflix.
  • Both companies' stocks rose Monday on news the settlement cleared the path to closing.

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) Streaming integration of Max and Paramount+ into one bundle will test subscriber growth, while a flood of theatrical releases may pressure box office returns or trigger production cutbacks depending on debt servicing needs.

Bitcoin and crypto stocks rally despite stalled regulation

  • Bitcoin rose to its highest level since January, gaining roughly 6-7% to trade above $85,000-$87,000.
  • Rally came even after the Clarity Act (proposed U.S. legislation to establish a regulatory framework for crypto) failed to advance in Congress, showing bitcoin can gain without new regulatory support.
  • MicroStrategy stock jumped 8-9% after disclosing additional bitcoin purchases and preferred stock buybacks, pushing the company back into positive year-to-date territory.
  • Crypto-linked stocks broadly outperformed: Coinbase up about 10% and Robinhood up about 11% over five days, while bitcoin balance-sheet holders like MicroStrategy rallied around 28%, more than three times the gain of trading exchanges.

Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen) The gap between balance-sheet holders and exchanges indicates the rally is driven by bitcoin's price appreciation itself rather than increased trading activity or user adoption.
