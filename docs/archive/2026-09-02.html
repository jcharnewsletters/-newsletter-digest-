US-Iran Military Escalation Drives Oil Prices and Diesel Crisis

  • Two supertankers were struck by projectiles near the Strait of Hormuz (chokepoint carrying ~20% of global oil supply), reigniting fears of broader conflict.
  • US launched a second round of strikes on Iran within three days; Iran retaliated against US bases and accused US of hitting a wedding ceremony.
  • Crude oil hit five-week highs, with Brent near $94/barrel and WTI above $90, a roughly 6% jump.
  • Diesel crack spread (refiners' profit margin on turning crude into diesel) hit a record $106/barrel, pushing retail diesel to $5.63/gallon, nearing the 2022 record of $5.80.
  • National gas prices stayed above $4/gallon for the entire month of August for the first time ever.
  • Trump met with major refiner executives (Marathon, Valero, Phillips 66, Chevron, PBF Energy) to discuss expanding capacity and easing regulations, but refiners cited high costs, long timelines (3-5 years), and blamed EPA biofuel blending mandates for elevated pump prices.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent claimed the Strait of Hormuz will become a 'worthless piece of water' once alternate Gulf pipeline routes are built.
