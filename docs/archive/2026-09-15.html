10-Year Treasury Yield Surges Past 5%, Fed Rate Hike Expected Wednesday

  • Rising yields are pressuring stock futures and equities: S&P 500 fell 0.5%, Nasdaq 0.6%, Dow 0.3%, with strategists warning of an 8-10% pullback risk.

Global Oil Supply Shock: Saudi Pipeline Attack and Red Sea Disruptions
