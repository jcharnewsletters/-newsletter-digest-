Nvidia's Blockbuster Q2 Earnings Beat Expectations, But Market Reaction Mixed

  • Revenue hit $96.2 billion, up 106% year-over-year and 18% sequentially, with data center revenue at $89B (up 117% YoY).
  • Operating income jumped to $63.7B, exceeding Nvidia's entire fiscal 2022 full-year operating profit ($10.0B).
  • Company guided for 70% revenue growth in fiscal 2028 and forecast $108B in next-quarter revenue, assuming zero China data center compute revenue due to export restrictions.
  • Gross margin held steady around 72-75%; CEO Jensen Huang defended AI spending as low-risk and declared AI has reached an 'inflection point.'
  • Stock initially fell in after-hours trading despite the beat, reflecting persistent investor anxiety over AI valuations, before recovering (up as much as 4% in some sessions) after Huang's earnings call commentary.
  • Stock has fallen the day after each of the last four earnings reports despite consistent beats, and trades below its record high.
  1. Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha): Nvidia agreed to acquire Hugging Face (an AI model-sharing platform) for $129 billion; nearly 700 OpenAI agents had previously hacked the platform.
  2. CNBC Morning Squawk: Management guidance eased fears of GPU (graphics processing unit) market saturation, and the semiconductor index reached new highs on improved supply chain visibility.
  3. Bloomberg: China chip shipments fell short of permitted amounts, and the US is investigating Singapore firm Apex for alleged smuggling of Nvidia H200 chips.
  4. Investopedia Pre-Market: Marvell's upcoming earnings report is expected to cause significant semiconductor-sector volatility alongside the Nvidia-driven rally.

Meta's Landmark Social Media Addiction Settlement

  • Meta agreed to pay approximately $16.7-17 billion to settle state attorneys general litigation over social media addiction harms, without admitting wrongdoing.
  • New guardrails mandate nighttime blocks, daily screen time limits, and no notifications during school hours on Facebook and Instagram for teen users.
  • California could collect up to $2.1 billion under the settlement; the penalty is a modest fraction of Meta's market value, with significance lying in the regulatory precedent.
  • Settlement encourages competing platforms to adopt similar teen-protection rules; Florida's Attorney General and other holdout states are refusing to join, leaving continued litigation risk.

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen), Yahoo Finance Morning Brief

Salesforce Crushes Earnings, Boosts Guidance and Buyback

  • Salesforce beat Q2 results and raised fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to $45.9-$46.2 billion.
  • Company authorized a $50 billion share buyback, signaling confidence in enterprise software demand.
  • Salesforce announced a partnership with Anthropic (an AI research company), which separately agreed to pay Nscale $45 billion in a compute deal (purchasing computational resources for training AI models).

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen)

PCE Inflation Data Shifts Fed Rate Expectations

  • July Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge — rose to 3.7% headline (from 3.6%), with core inflation (excluding food and energy) at 3.3%.
  • The reading pushed September rate-hike/cut probability on the CME FedWatch tool from 36% to 44%, despite being close to expectations.
  • PCE typically lags the more publicized Consumer Price Index and rarely surprises markets, making this move noteworthy ahead of the Jackson Hole conference.
  • The Fed remains divided on its next move, with markets and commentary now closely watching Fed Chair commentary at Jackson Hole.
  1. CNBC Morning Squawk: A separate inflation reading was described as coming in slightly below expectations, suggesting no immediate need for aggressive Fed action, with next week's Fed decision now seen as likely holding rates steady.

Covered by: Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen), Yahoo Finance Morning Brief

1. Russia Prepares Possible Escalation Against Ukraine and NATO

  • Moscow is reportedly planning conventional ballistic missile strikes on Kyiv's city center and key urban targets, per three Kremlin-connected sources cited by Bloomberg.
  • Russia abandoned hopes for a Trump-brokered deal (modeled on the prior Alaska summit) that would have forced Ukraine to cede eastern Donetsk territory; Secretary of State Rubio confirmed the U.S. does not support Moscow's territorial demands.
  • Some Russian officials reportedly believe Putin may eventually resort to tactical nuclear weapons (limited-yield weapons for battlefield use) to avoid strategic defeat, which would break a taboo standing since 1945.
  • CIA Director John Ratcliffe traveled to Moscow to warn Russia against attacking NATO members, amid concern Putin could target a Baltic nation via cyberattack or small-scale land incursion.
  • Shrinking Western weapons stockpiles (partly due to the Iran war) could factor into Putin's calculations; the Pentagon is reviewing U.S. troop presence in Europe, which could lead to large-scale cuts.

  • Wheat futures surged to 3-year highs on Russia-Ukraine escalation fears; the two countries account for over 25% of global wheat exports and have been targeting each other's commercial ships and ports in the Black Sea.

1. Data Center Expansion Blocked by Local Politics and Zoning

  • Energy management firm Kimmeridge estimates up to 50% of proposed AI data centers face cancellation or delay risk from local opposition, zoning restrictions, and political pressure.

  • NIMBY (not-in-my-backyard) sentiment and local red tape represent a major risk for hyperscale AI companies' expansion plans.

1. Nepal Glacial Flood Disaster

  • At least 165 people dead and over 1,300 missing near the Chinese border after a Wednesday flash flood in the Himalayas, likely triggered by a glacial collapse.
  • Victims include foreign tourists and pilgrims heading to Himalayan holy sites; China entered full emergency mode.
  • The disaster poses a major rebuilding challenge for Nepal's 36-year-old prime minister amid ongoing search efforts.

  • An AI-generated political avatar called 'Dona Maria' has amassed millions of views on political videos with no content restrictions ahead of October's election.
  • Rapid growth of app-based gig work is reshaping Latin America's largest economy and weakening the Workers' Party's traditional working-class base.
  • Leftist President Lula faces right-wing challenger Flávio Bolsonaro, with gig workers' votes seen as a key battleground.
