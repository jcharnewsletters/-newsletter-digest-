  • Google signed a deal with Blue Origin to launch solar-powered AI chip satellites (part of Project Suncatcher) on the New Glenn rocket, targeting Q1 2029.
  • Each satellite will carry custom Tensor Processing Units (TPUs—specialized chips for AI math) equivalent to a terrestrial data center rack, operating in sun-synchronous orbit for near-constant solar power.
