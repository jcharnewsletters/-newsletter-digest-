Why the Current AI Buildout Differs from the 1999 Dot-Com Overbuild

  • Every AI chip (GPU) is deployed immediately upon arrival at 100% utilization, unlike the 1990s when companies built infrastructure like unused 'dark fiber' (unused cable laid before demand existed) before knowing how to use it.
  • AI deployment starts with roughly 95% of the population already using advanced technology daily, unlike the 1990s challenge of getting the entire population online for the first time.
  • Data centers take 2-3 years to build, a physical time lag that acts as a natural governor preventing infrastructure from outpacing demand before projects are operational.
  • Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta have committed to 5-10 year funding cycles rather than quarterly product cycles, stabilizing investment decisions.
  • SMH semiconductor ETF is up 78% over 12 months and has quadrupled the S&P 500's gains since ChatGPT's launch.
