Why Crypto Cards Cost ~2% for Cross-Border Spending

  • Crypto credit card fees abroad (~2%) stem from network assessment fees, FX spreads, and cross-border settlement costs that compound unless addressed.
  • Only a few issuers—Wirex, StraitsX, and Monavate—currently offer multi-currency settlement or local issuance at scale to reduce these fees.
  • Traditional stablecoin issuers earn revenue from idle reserve assets, discouraging them from enabling settlement use cases that would reduce those balances.
  • Open Standard's Open USD model instead ties issuer revenue to transaction volume, sharing reserve yield with developers and charging per-transaction fees, attracting strong institutional interest for card settlement and capital markets use cases.

OpenAI claims progress on a Millennium Prize math problem (disputed)

  • OpenAI used ~10,000 coordinated AI agents working in parallel for ~88 hours to explore proof strategies, sharing promising approaches across groups.
  • OpenAI has not named the problem, but Andrew Curran and others suggest it may be the Hodge Conjecture; other reports frame it as Navier-Stokes, so the exact problem remains unconfirmed.
  • OpenAI itself says the result needs independent review, formal verification, and outside mathematician approval (the 'Terence Tao plus-one' test) before it can be called solved.
  • Mathematicians including Terence Tao have flagged the claim as unverified; NYU's Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic's Levent Alpöge noted the breakthrough came after related unpublished research was uploaded into Codex (OpenAI's coding model), raising questions about whether AI did truly original work or completed a scaffolded solution.
  • Key technical advantage cited: checkability — thousands of agents can test proof ideas and code verification lets teams validate answers quickly, turning extra compute into more attempts at a provable result.

Staying Ahead Frames this as 'huge if true, not confirmed' and argues that even if genuine, near-term job risk from AI research capability remains low.
