August CPI report to determine Fed's September rate decision

  • Headline CPI expected around 3.4% year-over-year for August (steady vs. July), with monthly pace accelerating to 0.4% from July's 0.1%.
  • Core CPI (excludes volatile food and energy prices) forecast at 2.4% year-over-year, down slightly from July's 2.5%.
  • Sticky wholesale inflation (PPI, or Producer Price Index, which measures prices businesses receive for goods/services) confirmed price pressures are building, driven largely by energy and services costs.
  • Markets pricing 67% odds of a 25-basis-point rate hike (0.25 percentage point interest rate increase) at next week's Fed meeting, the highest odds of this rate cycle.
  • Fed officials say services and shelter inflation components will be the key drivers of their decision; most independent economists remain skeptical the Fed will hike, expecting it to wait for more data.
