Inflation data and rising Treasury yields keep Fed rate hikes in play

  • October CPI (Consumer Price Index, a measure of inflation) rose 0.4% month-over-month, beating the 0.3% consensus forecast and signaling persistent price pressures.
  • Markets now price in a possible 50-basis-point (0.5 percentage point) December rate hike, up from expectations of just 25 basis points (0.25 percentage points).
  • 10-year Treasury bond yield jumped 26 basis points to 5.14%, later hitting its highest level since October on hot wholesale price data.
  • Hotter-than-expected wholesale prices add to inflation concerns, reinforcing the case for continued Fed tightening despite earlier hopes for cuts.

Anthropic's upcoming IPO and AI ambitions

  • Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei told JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon the company aims to 'underpromise and overdeliver' ahead of next week's IPO (Initial Public Offering, when a private company first sells shares to the public).
  • The IPO is primarily intended to secure cash for computing infrastructure rather than to maximize valuation; reports indicate the targeted valuation has dropped below earlier $2 trillion hopes.
