03

Glamsterdam Upgrade Halted Over Critical Gas Mechanism Vulnerabilities

  • ChainSecurity identified flaws in EIP-8037/8038, proposals central to increasing Ethereum's gas limit (the maximum computational work allowed per block) from 60M to 200M, with at least 13 major DeFi protocols affected.
  • The vulnerability: EIP-8037's gas refill mechanism when storage slots are freed breaks the 1/64 gas guarantee in nested contract calls (a rule ensuring inner contract calls retain enough gas to run), letting attackers trigger outer-contract error handlers that lack gas to execute.
  • Ethereum Foundation and Ethlabs researchers deployed AI agents overnight to scan for vulnerable contracts, coordinate via GitHub issue comments and shared directories, and produce consolidated findings without human intermediation.
  • Core developers paused the launch date pending full impact analysis; the same agent-scanning framework is now running against EIP-8198 (Quick Slots, reducing slot time from 12 to 10 seconds) ahead of the Hegotá upgrade.
04

On-Chain Options Market Poised to Displace Traditional Derivatives

  • On-chain options currently hold under 1% of perpetual DEX volume despite representing 3x the futures volume in traditional finance, indicating massive growth potential.
  • 10x perpetual positions (futures contracts with no expiry, 10x leverage) carry a 47% liquidation probability over 180 days, making options structurally superior for multi-week trading theses needing downside protection.
  • Options sellers hedge on perpetual markets, expanding perpetual open interest (total value of outstanding contracts) rather than cannibalizing it; Deribit already shows $2B+ in futures OI sourced from options hedging.
  • September was the best on-chain options month on record at ~$5B notional volume, with Derive commanding 90%+ of on-chain premium ($18B notional traded, up 1,000%+ year-to-date); Hyperliquid, Derive, and Paradex are positioned as primary beneficiaries of projected growth to 5% of perp volume.

OpenAI's scientific breakthrough claims (math, computer science, physics)

  • OpenAI's system reportedly disproved the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH), a 1991 conjecture stating no fundamentally faster algorithm exists for Boolean satisfiability (checking if true/false switch combinations can satisfy a set of rules).
  • Lean, an automated proof-checking program (like a spellchecker for logic), verified the result on October 17; hundreds of papers rely on ETH as an assumption, so disproving it could reopen many 'impossible' problems.
  • Russell Impagliazzo and Ramamohan Paturi, two of ETH's three original authors, now lead a working group to verify whether the AI's disproof is valid.
  • OpenAI also announced findings in harmonic analysis (decomposing complex signals) and quantum field theory (physics framework for subatomic particles); a Nobel laureate group is reviewing the physics claim, and all results await independent verification.
  • OpenAI calls the manuscripts 'raw research data,' cautioning that turning a disproved ETH into faster real-world software will require significant further work.

How to test AI models on your own tasks

  • Pick an objectively gradable task (form-filling, spreadsheet cleaning, invoice logging) and run it three times each on a cheaper vs. pricier model, measuring accuracy and time per run.

PHP security crisis exposes decision-making paralysis

  • A known PHP vulnerability sat unpatched since 2024 because the foundation — run by one full-time employee, 14 part-time contractors, and 10 unpaid board members — couldn't agree on a solution under its consensus-based process.
  • AI researchers weaponized the flaw in August into a working attack enabling malicious code execution on servers; emergency fixes deployed Oct. 21 were followed by millions of attack attempts and confirmed breaches by Oct. 24.
  • The PHP Foundation has since shifted from consensus-based decisions to majority rule on hard calls to prevent future delays.
  • Lesson highlighted: technical capability to fix a problem means little without decision-making structures that can resolve trade-offs when consensus breaks down.
