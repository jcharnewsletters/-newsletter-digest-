Apple launches $2,000 iPhone Duo foldable phone

  • Apple unveiled the iPhone Duo, a foldable smartphone priced at $1,999–$2,000, marking its first major iPhone design shift in years.
  • Device features a 7.6-inch inner display and 5.4-inch cover screen; positioned by exec John Ternus as cleaner than rivals' 'two phones awkwardly stuck together.'
  • Foldables are just 2% of the global smartphone market; Counterpoint Research forecasts 6 million Apple units sold in 2026 (25% share, behind Samsung's 38%), generating roughly $14 billion in revenue (~3% of Apple's total), though that revenue won't post until 2027.
  • Strategy mirrors the classic 'Cook playbook': entering categories years after competitors (Samsung pioneered foldables six years ago) but refining execution to dominate.
  • Apple stock dipped immediately post-announcement, but Bank of America research suggests recovery typically occurs within 30-60 days; true test will be actual sales given inflation and chip-supply pressures.
  • Preorders begin in October.

Yahoo Finance Morning Brief Framed as a critical test of whether Apple can still redefine categories and reignite a stalled consumer upgrade cycle amid inflation.
