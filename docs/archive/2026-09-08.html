US-Canada Trade War Escalates

  • Canada imposed retaliatory tariffs of 15%-50% on hundreds of US goods (steel, aluminum, motorcycles, dairy), timed to hit before US midterm elections.
  • Tariffs hit hardest in Michigan and Ohio—states with heavy Canada trade and tight midterm Senate/House races.
  • PM Mark Carney is betting that standing firm against Trump will improve Ottawa's negotiating position long-term.
  • Trump retaliated by threatening to bar Canadian plane maker Bombardier from US sales and by targeting the Canadian dollar.
  • Deep economic interconnection creates risk: automakers (GM, Ford) rely on cross-border parts flow with Ontario; US refiners depend on discounted Canadian heavy crude; steelmakers (Nucor, Steel Dynamics) face input cost volatility.
  • Tariffs on Canadian vehicles/parts are set to phase in by 2027; overall resolution timeline remains unclear.
  • Seeking Alpha reader survey (1,470 respondents): 35% expect a mutual face-saving deal, 31% predict the US backs down due to supply-chain shocks and inflation risk; analysts frame the standoff as 'leverage and brinkmanship' rather than negotiation.

Oil Prices Surge on Middle East Escalation

  • Crude spiked roughly 1.5-2.7% across benchmarks: WTI hit $93.77-$93.97/barrel, Brent rose to about $98.62 and is approaching $100/barrel.
  • Fresh strikes hit Saudi Arabian oil facilities (including Aramco and Jizan refineries), forcing halts at several southern energy sites.
  • Iran has issued dual economic and military threats, while separately signaling openness to de-escalate tensions over the Strait of Hormuz (a chokepoint for about 21% of global oil supply) in exchange for sanctions relief talks.
  • Goldman Sachs flagged upside risk of Brent reaching $120/barrel if Iran-related disruptions persist.
  • US diesel prices hit an all-time high of $5.85/gallon (surpassing the June 2022 record), driven by cutoffs in Persian Gulf diesel supply and Ukrainian strikes on Russian refineries, which together supplied about one-third of global diesel exports in 2025; East Coast distillate stockpiles are at record lows heading into winter heating season.
