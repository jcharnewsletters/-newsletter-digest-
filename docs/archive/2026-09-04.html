August Jobs Report Looms, Estimates Vary Widely, Fed Rate Path in Focus

  • Economist estimates for August nonfarm payrolls (new jobs added, excluding farm workers) range widely: 55K (Seeking Alpha), 58K (Yahoo Finance), 65K (Opening Bell), reflecting genuine uncertainty.
  • Unemployment rate expected to hold around 4.1%-4.3% depending on source, with some seeing a tick up from 4.1% to 4.2%.
  • One-time distortions could skew the data: expiration of Temporary Protected Status (TPS, legal work permission for foreign nationals) for 330K Haitian immigrants (~200K with work permits) and recent meat-processing plant closures add 'noise' unrelated to real labor market health.
  • August has a 10-year history of downside surprises due to lower survey response rates, per KPMG's Diane Swonk, who warns the 55K consensus may be too optimistic.
  • Private hiring already hit a 7-month low, and August saw the slowest layoff rate in four years, suggesting a stable but cooling 'low hire, low fire' labor market echoed in July JOLTS data.
  • Wage growth (average hourly earnings) is expected to cool, easing inflation pressure and supporting the case for a Fed rate cut.
  • Markets are split on Fed action: bond markets price a 50-65% chance of a September rate cut (down from 65-85% two weeks earlier), with Fed officials divided between supporting employment (favoring cuts) and fighting inflation (favoring patience).
  • A stronger-than-expected report could delay cuts or even pressure a rate hike and push Treasury yields higher (10-year around 3.8-3.9%), while a weaker report could embolden the Fed to cut, with market swings of 2-4% possible either way.
  • Growth and tech stocks are seen as most sensitive to the outcome, since they benefit disproportionately from lower rates.

Nvidia Acquires Hugging Face for ~$13 Billion

  • Nvidia is acquiring Hugging Face, an open-weight AI platform (publicly available AI models anyone can use and modify), for approximately $13 billion ($12.9B-$13B depending on source).
  • Deal structured as $11.9 billion purchase price plus $1 billion in equity retention to keep new staff, making it Nvidia's second-largest acquisition ever.
  • Strategic shift: Nvidia moves beyond chip manufacturing into a full AI ecosystem play, aiming to foster developer tool creation and create new customers rather than solely selling hardware.
  • Comparisons drawn to historical industrial expansions (railroads, oil, gold), where dominant suppliers began financing ventures and connecting companies, not just supplying components.

Robotaxi Race Accelerates: Tesla Cybercab and Amazon's Zoox
