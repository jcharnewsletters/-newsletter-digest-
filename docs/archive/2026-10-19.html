French political and social unrest fuels fears of European debt contagion

  • Violent protests and riots broke out across French cities over the weekend, starting from cost-of-living and immigration grievances and escalating into looting, fires, hundreds of injured officers, and millions in business losses.
  • France's national debt stands at about 120% of GDP, with the government absorbing over 50% of private economic resources for entitlements and pensions, and borrowing costs have surged by the most among G7 nations since the Iran war.
  • The CAC 40 index fell roughly 1.4-1.5% as political gridlock in the National Assembly and a presidential election just six months away leave the government with few tools to address the crisis.
  • President Macron promised a 'hard line' response in a televised address Sunday night, though tensions flared even during the speech.
