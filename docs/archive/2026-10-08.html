The 'Slop Grenade' Problem: AI Output Without Rigor

  • BetterUp/Stanford research: 40% of U.S. desk workers receive a 'slop grenade' (a polished but nearly meaningless AI-generated document) monthly, costing about 2 hours each to decode.
  • Root cause: leaders mandate AI use without modeling good practice, creating a norm of 'use it' rather than 'use it well'\u2014worse when the slop comes from high-authority figures.
  • Fix: leaders should share one completed AI-assisted project showing their process, fact-checking, and remaining uncertainties, label drafts, invite critique, and avoid sending AI work that takes longer to read than it took to create.
