CFTC Chair's Know-Your-Agent Proposal for AI Traders

  • CFTC Chair proposes 'Know-Your-Agent' rules requiring traders to disclose AI and algorithmic trading systems to regulators, modeled on Know-Your-Customer (KYC) rules that verify a trader's identity and financial background.
  • Aims to close a regulatory gap where algorithmic orders are treated the same as manual trades despite differences in execution speed, volume, and market impact.
  • Targets risks from automated systems triggering unintended moves in leveraged derivatives (contracts that multiply gains/losses using borrowed money), including algorithmic liquidation cascades that amplify volatility.
  • Compliance costs could hit retail traders and smaller firms hardest, potentially consolidating the industry toward larger players with dedicated compliance infrastructure.
  • Definition of a regulated 'agent' remains unclear—must distinguish simple conditional orders from machine learning strategies and fully autonomous systems.
  • Enforcement mechanism (fines, trading suspensions, or deregistration) has not yet been specified.
