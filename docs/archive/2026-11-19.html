Anthropic's Record-Breaking IPO

  • AI company Anthropic (maker of the Claude chatbot) began trading on Nasdaq under ticker ANT, in the largest tech IPO on record.
  • Reported pricing and raise figures varied widely by outlet: CNBC cited $98/share raising $81.2B (~$980B valuation); Seeking Alpha cited $83/share raising $81.3B plus a $26B private placement for a $107.3B total deal (~$1.5T fully diluted valuation); Opening Bell cited $83/share raising ~$68.5B (~$1.08T valuation); Bloomberg cited a $107.3B combined public/private raise surpassing SpaceX's prior record.
  • The deal surpasses previous record IPOs (variously cited as Saudi Aramco's 2019 listing or SpaceX's private raise five months earlier).
  • Revenue is growing extremely fast (10x year-over-year to ~$16.3B per Seeking Alpha, or $17B per Opening Bell, roughly 12x prior year) but the company posted a massive net loss (~$80.7B per Seeking Alpha, ~$104B per Opening Bell), driven mainly by non-cash valuation/convertible-note charges rather than operating losses.
  • News of the IPO lifted Nasdaq futures (+2.1%) and broader tech and S&P 500 futures (+1.2%), reflecting investor enthusiasm for AI infrastructure plays.
  • The listing intensifies competition with OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Microsoft in the AI race, and Anthropic will be added to broad tech indices and potentially the S&P 500, giving institutional investors direct access.

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) Analysts flagged that Anthropic has $900.9B in compute (computing power/data center) commitments against only $35.3B trailing revenue—a fixed cost that doesn't shrink if sales disappoint.

Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen) The IPO is framed as Wall Street's largest-ever bet on 'artificial superintelligence' (AI systems that could exceed human capability across all domains).

Strong Retail Earnings Lift Target, Lowe's, TJX

  • Target posted $27.1 billion in quarterly sales (+7.1%), beat earnings with $2.65 EPS (including a $0.50 tariff-refund benefit), raised its full-year outlook, and saw comparable sales growth of 5.6%.
  • Target also reported a 5% jump in foot traffic, its largest gain since January 2022, attributed to a strategy of refreshing inventory late into December for last-minute holiday shoppers.
  • Lowe's comparable sales rose 1.8% to $22.76 billion quarterly; the company raised full-year guidance to ~$92.5 billion after prior weakness.
  • TJX (T.J. Maxx parent) reported comparable sales up 4% in Q3, revenue of $15.85 billion (topping estimates), raised full-year earnings guidance, and saw its stock jump 9%.
  • TJX's CEO credited the gains to stocking fresh inventory arriving within 10 days of Christmas, exploiting a gap left by competitors who had already sold through or marked down seasonal stock.

Yahoo Finance Morning Brief Target's CEO Michael Fiddelke specifically credited the late-inventory-refresh strategy for the foot-traffic gain; October retail data showed consumers pulling back on big purchases like cars and furniture while sustaining other spending.

Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen) Provided consolidated hard numbers across all three retailers (Target, Lowe's, TJX) in one roundup alongside broader market context.

Federal Reserve December Rate Decision Uncertainty

  • Fed officials are split on the size of a potential December rate move, with debate over whether to hike by the standard 25 basis points or a larger 50 basis point increase (a larger-than-normal rate hike).
  • New York Fed President John Williams ruled out a 50 bp hike, while Governor Chris Waller argued for one, saying skipping it now would force the Fed to hike again in January.
  • Minutes show almost all Fed officials judged further rate hikes would likely be appropriate; markets are fully pricing in a December hike, per Seeking Alpha, eliminating pause expectations.

Yahoo Finance Morning Brief Explained Waller's reasoning via 'forward guidance' (clear Fed communication about future policy plans) and noted Fed Chair Warsh has historically resisted forward guidance, creating a policy dilemma.

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) Stated markets have fully priced in a December hike, removing any expectation of a pause.
