Friday's Session and July Wrap: Amazon Soars, Apple Sinks

  • The S&P 500 rose 0.7% Friday, lifted by Amazon's fast-growing cloud business (AWS, which rents computing power to other companies) and Microsoft's disciplined spending.
  • The Dow finished July with slim gains while the Nasdaq and S&P 500 both closed the month lower.
  • Apple fell despite beating earnings expectations, hurt by disappointing China sales and a CEO warning about memory-chip shortages.
  • Amazon executives signaled a measured approach to spending even amid the AI boom.
  1. Yahoo Finance Morning Brief: Puts the Apple drop at nearly 10% following the memory-shortage warning.
  2. Investopedia Pre-Market: Frames the Friday moves as sector-level rotation among mega-cap tech names.

Treasury Yields Spike as Fed Holds Rates

  • 10-year Treasury yields (the interest rate on U.S. government debt maturing in 10 years) hit their highest level since January 2025.
  • Fed Chair Kevin Warsh held rates steady at 3.5%–3.75%, arguing that rising bond yields are themselves tightening financial conditions.
  1. Investopedia Pre-Market: Reads the yield move as markets pricing in higher interest rates ahead.
  2. Yahoo Finance Morning Brief: Evercore ISI's Krishna Guha called Warsh's messaging confusing, fueling "fears that he is a dove in hawk's clothing" (sounding lenient while claiming toughness).
