Binance Suffers Record $6.3 Billion Crypto Heist

  • Binance was hit by a $6.3 billion theft, the largest cryptocurrency heist in history, allegedly linked to North Korea.
  • About $3 billion has been frozen, but a significant portion of losses may be permanent; Binance says it will cover losses and is working with law enforcement.
  • The key unanswered question is whether AI played a role in executing the attack, which could have major implications for corporate security and insurance industries.
  • Bitcoin prices fell following the theft, raising concerns that AI-powered heists could become a routine corporate threat.

Google's AI Search Summaries Crush Website Referral Traffic

  • Google's shift from linking out to websites toward AI-generated summaries and chatbot answers is sharply reducing referral traffic across the internet.
  • Reddit CEO Steve Huffman said search referral traffic was down 'meaningfully' last quarter due to Google's AI-driven search mode.
  • The trend, dubbed 'Google Zero' (fear of search referral traffic approaching zero), is a widespread concern for content sites and businesses reliant on search traffic.
  • In response, Reddit plans to require user logins/accounts, shifting its strategy away from dependence on Google-driven traffic.
