Nvidia Q2 Earnings: AI Demand Test

  • Nvidia reports Q2 results after market close, with analysts expecting revenue around $92B, nearly 100% year-over-year growth (up from $46B a year ago).
  • Company has beaten earnings consensus in 12 of last 13 quarters and cleared its own revenue guidance midpoint for 15 straight quarters.
  • Stock has struggled recently — down about 11% from all-time high and enduring its longest losing streak since 2022 (7 straight down days) before recovering.
  • Key focus areas: data center revenue growth, profit margins, and forward guidance, especially around the 2027 transition to next-gen 'Vera Rubin' processors.
  • Analysts warn of a 'ticking time bomb': cheaper custom ASICs (application-specific integrated circuits — chips built by customers for specific tasks instead of buying general-purpose processors) could erode Nvidia's pricing power; OpenAI's in-house 'Jalapeño' chip reportedly already outperforms Nvidia's Blackwell in some benchmarks.
  • Options market prices a ±6.1% move by Friday, implying a post-earnings range of about $196–$222, with bullish call activity dominating.
  • Wall Street price targets mostly exceed $300/share, implying ~41% upside from Tuesday's close.
  • Nvidia is also backing $105B in financing for OpenAI's Ohio data center and mobilizing $500B in third-party AI infrastructure capital, while simultaneously raising AI server prices over 15% due to rising memory costs — raising 'circular financing' concerns where infrastructure spending depends on debt rather than proven revenue.
  1. Yahoo Finance Morning Brief: Software stocks have rallied sharply as chip stocks peaked, with investors rotating from hardware to AI application plays (e.g., CrowdStrike, Workday earnings this week).
  2. Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen): AMD is being floated as a potential 40% upside 'value play' as investors reposition around Nvidia earnings.
  3. Investopedia Pre-Market: Salesforce and CrowdStrike also report earnings the same day, adding to sector-rotation risk and opportunity.
  4. CNBC Morning Squawk: Analysts stress the stock's big year-to-date gains mean results must beat expectations to avoid a post-earnings selloff.

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), CNBC Morning Squawk, Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen), Yahoo Finance Morning Brief, Investopedia Pre-Market

Canada-U.S. Trade Tensions Escalate

  • Canada announced $20B in retaliatory tariffs on U.S. goods — including steel, seafood, dairy, appliances, agricultural equipment, and electronics — set to take effect September 8.
  • Move is in response to new U.S. tariffs; Prime Minister Mark Carney called it 'dollar-for-dollar' retaliation.
  • The U.S. is reportedly weighing additional trade penalties against Canada, with a White House official suggesting higher tariffs may follow.
  1. Bloomberg: Ontario Premier Doug Ford called for a truce with Trump personally while still backing Canada's retaliatory tariff plan.

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen), Bloomberg

Oil Prices Fall on Strait of Hormuz Developments

  • Crude oil fell about 2.7% to roughly $80.15 per barrel.
  • Iran and Oman are reportedly discussing temporarily reopening the Strait of Hormuz (a critical chokepoint through which about 20% of global crude oil, or global seaborne oil, passes) to Israel-bound traffic, easing supply concerns.
  1. Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen): Frames this as Iran threatening to close the strait specifically to Israel-bound oil, rather than a full reopening negotiation.
  2. CNBC Morning Squawk: Ties the oil price decline more broadly to weakening second-half 2026 global demand forecasts rather than the Hormuz talks alone.

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen), CNBC Morning Squawk

DICK'S Sporting Goods Plunges, Dragging Retail Sector

  • DICK'S Sporting Goods stock fell about 30-31% after missing Q2 earnings expectations and cutting its full-year outlook, citing fewer product launches and rival promotions.
  • Foot Locker also cut its outlook, and the spillover dragged down the broader sportswear and footwear sector.
  • Signals broader consumer spending weakness in discretionary retail.
  1. Yahoo Finance Morning Brief: Links the DICK'S drop to a broader consumer pullback: Conference Board Consumer Confidence Index hit a 7-month low in August, Walmart posted its slowest same-store sales growth since late 2020, and Lowe's/Home Depot reported customers cutting back on home projects.

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), Yahoo Finance Morning Brief

SoftBank Weighs Large Bond Sale Tied to OpenAI Investment

  • SoftBank is in talks with investment banks about a $10B–$20B bond offering to refinance a loan tied to its investment in OpenAI.
  • Move reflects a broader surge in AI-related fundraising despite growing concerns about mounting credit risk.

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), Bloomberg

Meta Discusses Settling Teen Social Media Addiction Lawsuit

  • Meta and U.S. state attorneys general are discussing a mid-trial settlement over allegations the company deliberately designed Facebook and Instagram to addict teenagers.
  • A trial loss could expose Meta to penalties as high as $1.4 trillion, nearly equal to the company's entire market value.

Covered by: Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha), Bloomberg

1. Delaware Becomes New Boomer Retirement Hotspot

  • Baby Boomers are increasingly retiring to Delaware for its low taxes and beaches as traditional destinations like Florida grow pricier (South Florida's Consumer Price Index up 36% since 2019, with homeowner insurance premiums more than double the national average).
