Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Faces Possible Ouster Amid Scandal

  • Nvidia's board is deliberating whether to remove CEO Jensen Huang following reports of an extramarital affair with Kimberly Powell, a senior Nvidia executive who reported directly to him, creating a conflict-of-interest concern.
  • This compounds earlier allegations that Huang had a separate affair with an alleged Chinese spy, raising national security concerns given Nvidia's role in US-China chip policy.
  • Nvidia shares fell sharply in premarket/after-hours trading (reported variously down 7% to roughly $160, and down about 25-30% over the past month, erasing around $1.5 trillion in market value).
  • No clear successor has been named; analysts speculate chief engineer Ian Buck or CFO Colette Kress could step in, while the board reportedly works with outside firms on a search.
  • Elon Musk said it would be 'an honor' to hire Huang if he departs; betting markets are pricing in his exit.
  • Rival chipmakers AMD and Broadcom (and Alphabet, per one report) are rallying on speculation they could gain market share if Nvidia's leadership is disrupted.
  • Morgan Stanley removed Nvidia from its top picks list and Baird downgraded the stock to Neutral, comparing Huang to the Icarus myth despite praising Nvidia's technical lead.
  • Analysts warn an 'espionage-tainted reputation' could linger for years regardless of legal outcomes, with potential FBI and Congressional inquiries looming.
  • The scandal arrives as a US-China trade truce is set to expire in January, complicating matters since Huang had served as an informal diplomat on chip trade policy (including lobbying for and against China export restrictions on H20/H200 chips).

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) Yorkville Ives estimates Nvidia's fair value would fall 25% to $225/share without Huang versus $300/share if he stays; 59% of Seeking Alpha subscribers had expected him to stay.

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) Huang allegedly donated $25 million to the Trump administration's ballroom project, raising crony capitalism concerns given his access on China policy.

Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen) Despite the crisis, Nvidia shares actually rose 2.9% to $173.73 ahead of its November 17 earnings report, diverging from the broader AI sector recovery; the company represents about 8% of the S&P 500.

Bloomberg Nvidia's 10-member board includes members with backgrounds ranging from Taco Bell international operations to academic research, and must decide whether to remove the only CEO the company has ever had, who oversaw a 700,000% share price increase.

Yahoo Finance Morning Brief The New York Times broke the affair story while the Wall Street Journal reported on the board's ouster deliberations; Nvidia stock is down roughly 25% since early October.

CoreWeave Earnings Beat Sparks AI Infrastructure Sector Rally

  • CoreWeave (an AI cloud computing infrastructure company) reported Q3 revenue of $3.66 billion, up 168% year-over-year and ahead of analyst estimates.
  • Stock surged about 24-25% in a single day, its largest gain since June 2025, despite having fallen roughly 40% in the prior month on default insurance cost spikes; shares remain about 56% below their May peak of $137.98.

  • The earnings beat reignited confidence across AI infrastructure names, lifting AMD (up 36.7%), Nebius (up 28.5%), Broadcom, Intel, TSMC, ASML, Micron, and SK Hynix.
  • The market's fear gauge VIX (a measure of expected S&P 500 volatility) dropped to 17.49 from 28.18 in one week, and the S&P 500 recaptured all of November's losses, gaining 1.3% on the day.
