Anthropic's Claude Designs 211 Scorpion Antivenom Candidates

  • Citrini Research, a five-person non-scientist investment firm, used Claude to generate over 300,000 candidate proteins (molecules designed to bind and neutralize toxins) in 64 hours based on just five prompts.
  • 211 designs were sent to external labs (Twist Bioscience and Adaptyv Bio) for binding tests; 17 successfully bound to individual scorpion toxins.
  • Project used 367 prompts, roughly 10,000 H100 GPU-hours (a unit measuring AI computing time), and a $200K budget, compressing about a year of research into days.
  • None of the candidates have been tested for actual venom neutralization, and no animal studies, dosing, or safety testing have occurred, so a viable cure remains unproven.
  • Citrini called the 'universal antivenom' goal unsuccessful for now but said the experiment shows AI has shifted the frontier of what's possible in drug discovery for non-experts.

The Rundown AI Citrini conducted the work at Anthropic's own offices after security restrictions blocked them from using their own computers.

Meta Muse Share Links Exposed via Public DNS Records
