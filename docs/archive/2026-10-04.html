Medieval Potion Offers New Antibiotic Strategy

  • Scientists recreated Bald's eyesalve, a 10th-century remedy made from garlic, onion, bovine bile, and wine
  • Testing found the compound damages bacterial membranes, blocks cell-to-cell communication, and weakens biofilms (protective structures bacteria form to resist treatment)
  • Bacteria took significantly longer to develop resistance to the medieval remedy compared to modern antibiotics
  • Researchers plan to develop a lab-made version of the key ingredients rather than suggesting home brewing
04

Humidity-Powered Wallpaper Could Run Smart Home Devices

  • Scientists developed paper-based wallpaper packed with tiny moisture-electric generators (devices that extract water vapor from the air and convert it into electrical voltage)
  • A full-scale sheet containing 1,596 units produced enough power to run a wireless keyboard while simultaneously lowering room humidity
  • The system cannot yet power large appliances but could potentially keep battery-free sensors, thermostats, and other small smart-home devices running indefinitely
  • The technology represents a step toward harvesting ambient energy from everyday environments

OpenAI launches Dots, a personal AI agent platform

  • Dots is OpenAI's new personal AI agent platform, described by ChatGPT lead Tibo Sottiaux as the company's biggest bet, marking a shift from chat-based AI to autonomous agents that handle real tasks on the internet.

Agents predicted to dominate future internet traffic

  • OpenAI's ChatGPT leader predicts AI agents will soon handle most internet actions and transactions, shifting interactions from direct human-computer use to agent-to-service communication.
  • This requires new integration patterns and APIs, and creates opportunities for builders who design systems with agent capabilities in mind.
03

Traditional agent-building techniques becoming obsolete

  • Current popular methods—explicit loops (step-by-step instruction patterns), computational graphs (flowcharts of logic), and hand-tuned workflows—are described as temporary phases that next-generation agents will replace with more emergent, learned behaviors.
  • Builders heavily invested in loop- and graph-based architectures may find their approaches outdated as agent technology matures.
04

AI era shifting in-demand job skills

  • Skills like prompt engineering (crafting text inputs to guide AI output) are declining in demand, while multi-step reasoning and context window management (handling the amount of information an AI can consider at once) are trending up.

The 'airplane manual' trick for concise AI responses
