Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Resigns Amid Scandal and Export Control Controversy

  • Jensen Huang, Nvidia's founder and only-ever CEO for 30-33 years, resigned effective immediately after reports of multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman US intelligence allegedly identifies as a Chinese spy.
  • CFO Colette (also referred to as Nicole) Kress, who has a finance/MBA background rather than engineering, becomes interim CEO and will lead the company through next week's earnings report.
  • Nvidia's board stated it found 'no reason to believe that national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised,' despite the espionage allegations.
  • Banned Nvidia chips (semiconductors restricted from export to China under US sanctions) were traced to a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, a Chinese entrepreneur; Singapore has charged a fifth person in a related export case.
  • Stock initially plunged as much as 14% before closing down 4.3%, as retail investors bought the dip in what one analyst called 'one of the more epic buy-the-news events in history'; shares are now down 25% over the past month.
  • At least six Wall Street analysts downgraded Nvidia, with Dan Ives cutting his price target from $300 to $225; however, Nvidia trades at just 11x forward 2027 earnings, cheaper than peers like Broadcom, TSMC, Alphabet, Microsoft, and AMD.
  • Lawmakers are demanding accountability, with Huang agreeing to testify before Congress under oath before Thanksgiving alongside Commerce Secretary Howard/Marco Lutnick regarding alleged illegal chip exports.
  • Silicon Valley is questioning whether Lutnick did enough to prevent advanced AI chips from reaching China, and the fallout could accelerate China's drive for semiconductor self-sufficiency.

CNBC Morning Squawk Congressional demand for joint testimony from Lutnick and Huang signals lawmakers view this as a high-level governance failure requiring accountability from both private sector and government officials.

Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen) Washington lawmakers are preparing to add the AI Overwatch Act, Chip Security Act, and MATCH Act to this year's defense bill ahead of Trump's planned meeting with Xi Jinping; Chinese AI firms are accelerating purchases of Nvidia chip capacity overseas before potential new export restrictions.

SpaceX Attempts Historic Full Rocket Reusability

  • SpaceX will attempt tonight (5:45-7:00 PM ET) to recover an entire Starship rocket for the first time, capturing the first stage (booster) minutes after liftoff and the second stage on Saturday—a feat never before accomplished.
  • Previous reusable rocket efforts recovered only first-stage boosters; second stages were left unrecovered because adding heat shields, landing legs, and grid fins would consume too much payload capacity relative to the booster's cost.
  • Starship's large size and cargo capacity make full reusability economically viable, potentially enabling it to eventually replace Falcon rockets for commercial and government missions.
  • Full reusability would support Elon Musk's long-term vision of lunar landings, point-to-point Earth transport, and Mars colonization by significantly reducing launch costs.
