Calls for AI Slowdown Spark Global Political Divide

  • Elon Musk and Sam Altman publicly backed the call for a slower, safety-first pace of AI development.
  • President Trump rejected the slowdown proposal, saying 'Whoever wins AI wins,' arguing that ceding ground to China would be reckless; he offered no specific policy in response.

  • U.S. lawmakers offered only vague responses: House Speaker Mike Johnson proposed a single meeting with industry leaders, while Democrat Hakeem Jeffries pushed for faster but unspecified action.
