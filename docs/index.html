Anthropic's record IPO loses steam amid broader tech selloff

  • Anthropic priced its IPO at $83/share, raising $81.3 billion—the largest IPO on record, surpassing SpaceX's $75 billion June offering.
  • Stock opened around $111.50, briefly valuing the AI company at $2 trillion fully diluted (accounting for all potential share conversions) before settling near $1.27 trillion market cap.
  • Shares closed up 17.1% on debut day, but slipped on day two as broader tech/AI stocks sold off, with Dow, S&P 500, and Nasdaq all falling over 2% the day Anthropic began trading.
  • Combined wealth of Anthropic founders and employees now exceeds that of one-third of U.S. households.
  • Retail investor enthusiasm was notably weaker than for SpaceX: net buying of just $19 million in the first 30 minutes (vs. SpaceX's $26 million), widening to $37 million vs. $99 million after three hours, despite higher total trading volume ($820 million vs. $669 million) suggesting more tactical selling.
  • S&P 500 fell for 6 consecutive trading days, with futures plunging further Friday morning; Nasdaq futures were down roughly 1.4-1.7%.
  • Taiwan's benchmark stock index posted its worst week since 1991 amid a global AI sector selloff.
  • Gold fell 2.6% to $3,590.60 and Bitcoin dropped 3.5% to $61,738, reflecting broad risk-off sentiment across asset classes.
  • DayOne Data Centers postponed a multibillion-dollar IPO, citing concerns over elevated AI infrastructure valuations (the price investors assign to companies).

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) Nasdaq dominated 2026 IPO activity broadly, raising $201.8B across 64 listings (10x NYSE's $20.8B across 42 listings), with NDAQ stock up 21% year-over-year partly due to hosting both Anthropic's and SpaceX's mega-IPOs.

Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen) Anthropic's debut coincided with a short-seller report on AMD alleging improper firmware sales to a blacklisted Chinese firm, sending AMD down 7.7% and compounding AI-sector headwinds the same day.

Yahoo Finance Morning Brief Anthropic's valuation intensifies S&P 500 concentration risk, as the top 10 companies now represent 40% of index value; the equal-weighted S&P 500 is up 11% this year vs. just 6% for the market-cap-weighted version.

Bloomberg Global equities are on track for their longest losing streak in more than three years as the AI trade unravels, with Anthropic's IPO failing to lift Asian markets on day two.

CNBC Morning Squawk Framed the move as a broad 'tech wreck' dragging down both established tech firms and newly public companies like Anthropic simultaneously.
