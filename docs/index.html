Trump considers/backs US diesel export ban amid record prices

  • Diesel prices hit a record $6.53/gallon Tuesday, up 77% year-over-year, with California ($8.44/gal) and Washington ($7.47/gal) highest.
  • Trump voiced support for banning diesel exports; Treasury Secretary Bessent said the idea is under feasibility study.
  • US supplies about 1.5 million of the 8 million barrels traded globally each day (roughly 20% of global diesel trade); cutting exports could force refiners to reduce output across all fuel types, potentially raising rather than lowering US prices long-term.
  • Mexico and Latin America depend heavily on US diesel for agricultural transport; low Mexican inventories could cause physical fuel shortages within days, risking food-export disruption and higher US grocery prices.
  • Europe, already in a fuel crisis, could lose the equivalent of 1/7 of EU diesel demand (500,000 barrels/day) if US exports are cut, forcing buyers to scramble for alternatives.

SEC's new tokenized equities exemption locks out current market leaders

  • The exemption restricts trading to permissioned AMM venues (automated market makers, where prices adjust algorithmically based on supply and demand) requiring KYC/KYB (Know Your Customer/Know Your Business identity verification) for both liquidity providers and traders — excluding capital-efficient CLOB platforms (centralized limit order books, where traders submit specific price and quantity orders).
  • Only SEC-registered tokenized NMS stocks qualify, disqualifying every existing offering from Robinhood Crypto, Coinbase, Binance, Ondo, and xStocks.
  • Trading volume is capped at 0.25% of a stock's prior-month average daily share volume; the current $3 billion tokenized equity market is just 0.001% of the $70 trillion total equity market.
  • Operators must grant open-ended SEC examination rights and give 30 days' advance notice before launch, and investors in these tokenized securities lose the best-execution protections standard on traditional exchanges.
