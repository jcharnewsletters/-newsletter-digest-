First New Feline Species in Over a Century

  • Genetic testing confirmed the tilcayo—a Bolivia cloud forest wildcat smaller than a housecat with leopard-like spots—is a distinct species that diverged roughly 1.4 million years ago
  • This is the first formally identified new cat species (family Felidae) in more than 100 years, sparking major social media attention with 44 million views of footage showing the animal
02

T. Rex Body Temperature Redefined

  • New geochemistry technique analyzing fossilized teeth pinned the T. rex's body temperature at approximately 97°F (36°C), remarkably close to human body temperature
  • Previous belief that T. rex was reptilian and cold-blooded is incorrect—the dinosaur was warm-blooded (meaning it generated its own heat internally rather than relying on external warmth), enabling a more active lifestyle and wider geographic range since it didn't need sun exposure to regulate temperature
03

Human-Mouse Brain Hybrid Research Advances

  • Stanford team transplanted human brain organoids (lab-grown clusters of human brain cells) into mice engineered to lack their own cortex, creating animals whose brains contain millions of living human neurons
  • Despite the human neural tissue, the mice show typical mouse behavior and cognition on all tests performed so far; the research is already helping scientists study conditions like frontotemporal dementia
  • Creators are pushing for stronger ethical oversight before similar research expands to other animal species

U.S. Deploys Space-Based Military Weapons

  • Air Force Secretary Troy Meink made the first public acknowledgment that the U.S. has deployed space-based weapons designed to defend American military assets in space
  • This admission follows years of quiet preparation against similar moves by Russia and China, as satellites become critical infrastructure for GPS, communications, and other essential systems
  • Outer space could become a new military front as nations weaponize orbital assets

Neuralink Brain Implant Enables Speech Recovery

  • A trial participant with severe speech loss used Neuralink's N1 brain implant (a device surgically placed in the brain to read neural signals) to convert thoughts into audible words, telling his wife 'I love you' for the first time in years (16 million views)
  • The VOICE system decodes neural activity associated with speaking and can reportedly recreate elements of a user's pre-illness voice
  • The technology remains investigational without regulatory approval; development highlights potential to restore communication for paralyzed or speech-impaired patients
