US Budget Deficit Soars to Nearly $2 Trillion in Fiscal 2026

  • CBO reports $2 trillion deficit for fiscal year ending Sept. 30: government spent $7.4T (up 6% year-over-year) while collecting only $5.4T in revenue (up 3%).
  • Interest payments on the national debt now exceed the entire defense budget and every spending category except Social Security.
  • Deficits this large typically occur only during deep recessions or wartime, not sustained economic expansions.
  • Contributing factors include major tax breaks, rising Medicare costs from an aging population, lost tariff revenue after a Supreme Court trade ruling, and ongoing infrastructure spending.
  • Neither political party is addressing root causes; real fixes would require entitlement reform and tax increases rather than revenue cuts.
  • Treasury Secretary Bessent points to 3% economic growth as the way forward, but elevated post-pandemic inflation complicates that path.

Ethereum Researcher Warns AI Could Break Wallet Cryptography (ECDSA)

  • Ethereum researcher Justin Drake warns AI advances may enable breaking ECDSA (Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm, the cryptographic method many wallets use to protect private keys—the secret codes proving ownership and authorizing transactions) within months, not years.
  • Vulnerability stems from elliptic curves' algebraic structure potentially being exploitable via AI-discovered mathematical techniques, possibly enabling private key recovery on GPU clusters.
  • Recommended defenses: move assets to fresh addresses, rotate keys regularly, avoid broadcasting transactions that expose public keys, and move multisig confirmations offchain.
  • Ethereum roadmap is already shifting toward hash-based cryptography (SPHINCS+ and WOTS, cryptographic signature schemes believed resistant to these attacks), dropping lattice-based and ECDSA schemes; Drake urges accelerating this transition.
  • Industry reaction is split: a Coinbase cryptographer dismissed the warning as unfounded ("FUD") citing no evidence of weakened elliptic-curve security, while Dragonfly and the Solana Foundation view the risk as credible, noting Solana's Ed25519 signature scheme has a path to migrate to quantum-resistant alternatives.

CMC Spotlight Frames the issue broadly as affecting multiple wallet implementations and platforms, with security researchers urging wallet providers to immediately audit their cryptographic implementations.

TLDR Crypto Notes that OpenAI's 722 published mathematical results contain no cryptographic breakthroughs, which Drake suggests could indicate government suppression of cryptanalysis research; also covers Vitalik's parallel argument that lattice-based cryptography (ML-DSA, LWE) faces similar erosion risk as RSA did under historical optimization attacks, and that public-key encryption cannot be built from hashes alone, leaving secure messaging and VPNs with unresolved security problems beyond blockchains.
