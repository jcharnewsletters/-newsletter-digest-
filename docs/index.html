Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, Sparking Record-Breaking Rally

  • Nvidia's board removed Huang around Nov. 12-17 citing personal conduct issues, then reversed course on Nov. 23 and reinstated him, with board chair Harvey Jones calling him 'the right person to lead'.
  • Stock surged 31% on the reinstatement news, adding roughly $952 billion to $1 trillion in market value in a single day—the largest one-day market-cap gain for any stock on record, more than double Microsoft's prior $450 billion record.
  • President Trump said he personally called into the board meeting and 'convinced' directors to reinstate Huang, framing him as a 'national treasure' essential to national security and the American economy.
  • The rally helped drive the S&P 500 up 4.7% and Nasdaq Composite up 6.5%—the market's best day since April 2025—recovering much of the roughly $1.2 trillion in Nvidia value lost over the prior week.
  • Huang still faces Congressional testimony next month and an ongoing external investigation into foreign intelligence and export-control allegations; Sen. Elizabeth Warren called the reinstatement 'reckless' and demanded answers.
  • Analysts criticized the board's flip-flopping as a governance failure ('clown-like' response), and Bloomberg reported the board did not consult senior leadership before reversing its decision.
  • Markets pulled back the next day as enthusiasm faded: Nvidia and other AI stocks led declines, with futures down (Dow -0.5%, S&P 500 -0.9% to -1%, Nasdaq -1.7% to -2%), and Monday's rally is now seen as one of the strongest single days in market history, prompting talk of consolidation.

Opening Bell Daily (Phil Rosen) The Nasdaq-100 ETF (QQQ) opened nearly 8% higher—its largest opening gain since launching in 1999—while the iShares Semiconductor ETF opened 15% higher; European stocks, gold, and bitcoin also rallied in a broad risk-on move.
