SpaceX's Starship Catch: A Reusability Milestone

  • Mechanical capture avoids damage from traditional splashdown landings, cutting refurbishment time and cost.

How AI Learns Like a Sleeping Baby

  • Princeton researchers fed an AI model home recordings from a baby in chronological order, but by simulated age 2 it couldn't recognize a single word.
  • Adding a 'replay' step at the end of each simulated day—where the AI reviewed prior days' audio—let it recognize thousands of words across eight different homes by the same age.
  • The technique mimics how human babies may use sleep to consolidate memories, suggesting replay prevents an AI from forgetting earlier training while learning new patterns.
  • This method lets researchers study how sleep aids infant learning without fully retraining the model from scratch, addressing a key challenge for continuously improving AI systems.

Adobe is opening Photoshop to your AI agent

  • Keeping native project files editable after agent edits matters because AI outputs often need refinement, and pros require precise control for final delivery.

Meta's Muse: How the #1 App Store app was built and launched

  • Muse is the #1 App Store app and the fastest-growing AI consumer product ever, led since inception by VP of Product Mona Sarantakos
  • Originated as a weekend project using OpenClaw (an open-source AI agent framework, a toolkit for building autonomous AI assistants) running on a Raspberry Pi (a low-cost mini computer)
  • Team deliberately delayed public launch by 7 months despite having an early-mover advantage over competitors
  • Development was shaped by input from Nat Friedman (GitHub CEO), Alex Wang (Anthropic co-founder), and Mark Zuckerberg
  • Team uses 'agent swarms' (multiple coordinated AI systems working together to analyze data) to surface user behavior insights at scale instead of relying on small samples
  • This process revealed a primary use case the founding team had not originally predicted
  • Two specific behavioral levers were identified as key drivers of user activation (sign-up and engagement) for AI agents, separating successful products from unsuccessful ones
  • Jolly, originally just a placeholder avatar, became an iconic brand element central to the product's identity and adoption
  • Sarantakos names deep product expertise as the number one hiring criterion for PMs in the current AI era
