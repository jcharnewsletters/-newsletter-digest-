Rising Interest Rates Revalue Stock Market

  • The 10-year Treasury yield touched 5.33% this week and remains above 5%, increasing the cost of capital for borrowing.
  • Higher bond yields give investors a lower-risk alternative to stocks, forcing equities to compete on fundamentals rather than growth momentum.
  • Debt servicing costs on the U.S. government's growing debt pile rise significantly with each percentage-point increase in yields.

AI Stock Valuations Detached from Reality

  • CrowdStrike trades at 43.5x forward price-to-sales (annual revenue divided by stock market value) and a 209x forward P/E ratio (annual earnings forecast divided by stock price), above its 2021 peak.
  • These multiples suggest the market is pricing in extraordinary future growth that looks unlikely given the macro environment.
  • AMD and other AI-related stocks are reaching record highs despite macro headwinds, suggesting bubble-like behavior unsustainable against higher rates and slower earnings growth.

Debt Crisis Concerns Return to Mainstream

  • Harvard economist Kenneth Rogoff said in a recent interview that debt crisis elements are now present in the U.S., a shift from his previous view.
