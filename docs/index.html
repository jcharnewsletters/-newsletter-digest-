Apple's Q4 Earnings: Strong Results but Margin Concerns Pressure Stock

  • Apple posted record September-quarter revenue of $114.1 billion (beating estimates of $113.4 billion), with EPS of $2.10 topping the $1.99 estimate.
  • iPhone sales grew over 17% year-over-year to a September-quarter record, Greater China sales jumped 21%, and Services revenue hit an all-time high of $31.1 billion.
  • Despite beating estimates, Apple shares fell as investors focused on weaker profit-margin guidance, raising fears of profit pressure across the tech sector and dragging Dow futures lower.
  • Apple raised prices (e.g., $100 more on iPhone 18, $300 more on MacBook Pro) to offset higher input costs like memory chips, and still grew revenue 12%, showing strong pricing power and resilient consumer demand.
  • Apple's capital expenditures are remarkably low (just $9 billion over the last four quarters, under 2% of revenue) compared to AI-spending peers like Meta (43% of revenue) and Microsoft (39%), making it an outlier in the 'Magnificent 7' group of top tech stocks.
  • Apple stock is up about 24% year-to-date, the best-performing Magnificent 7 stock besides Nvidia, more than double the S&P 500's gain.
