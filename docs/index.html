SpaceX-Tesla $3.5 Trillion Merger Exploration

  • Elon Musk confirmed both companies formed independent director committees (groups of board members without conflicts of interest) to explore a merger that could create a combined entity worth $3.5 trillion, potentially the largest corporate deal ever.
  • Deal would merge SpaceX's rockets, satellites, and AI/data center business with Tesla's EVs, autonomous driving, and robotics.
  • Musk said the idea has 'a lot of merit' but nothing is certain; a Tesla shareholder vote (50% approval) is required, and Musk is excluded from committee discussions due to conflict of interest.
  • Merger would face intense regulatory scrutiny; analysts note SpaceX has more growth avenues than Tesla, and the deal could give Tesla liquidity while affecting SpaceX's valuation short-term.
  • Musk says merger committees are 'unconflicted' and continue working through details even as Tesla prepares its own earnings report.
