July retail sales fall 0.6% as consumer sentiment sours

  • Retail sales dropped 0.6% month-over-month in July, the sharpest decline since May 2025, versus expectations of +0.1% growth.
  • Online sales fell 2.2% and auto dealership sales fell 2%, pointing to broad-based spending weakness.
  • University of Michigan consumer sentiment (a monthly survey of how households feel about the economy) fell sharply in August on war, higher bond yields and inflation worries.
  • Declines in sentiment were steepest among older, lower-income and non-college-educated consumers.
  • Only 8% of consumers expect their income to outpace inflation over the next year.
  1. Yahoo Finance Morning Brief: Frames the gap as 'stocks up, vibes down,' with the S&P 500 nearing a 7,800 record even as household confidence erodes.

Wall Street Breakfast (Seeking Alpha) 5

1. The nickel now costs 13.31 cents to make

  • The US Mint spends 13.31 cents to produce and distribute each nickel worth 5 cents, a loss of more than 8 cents per coin.
  • Penny production already stopped last year as copper, zinc and nickel prices climbed.
  • Dimes and quarters still earn positive seigniorage (the profit between production cost and face value) and have subsidized penny and nickel losses, but that model is breaking down.

2. Common Cents Act advances in the Senate

  • The bill formally ends penny production and sets standardized rounding rules so cash prices round to the nearest nickel.
  • It authorizes Treasury to reformulate the nickel with cheaper metals instead of a proposed 10-year production freeze; the House passed similar framework language.
  • Precedent: the half-cent was scrapped in 1857, silver was removed from coins in 1965, and the penny switched from copper to zinc in 1982.

3. Jane Street lost $15 billion in July

  • The trading firm's loss followed the 'Situational Awareness' AI-driven market disruption, exposing systemic risk in algorithmic (computer-run) trading strategies.

4. Stripe to buy OpenRouter for over $7 billion

  • The payments company is consolidating AI model routing and infrastructure (software that directs requests to different AI models) into its platform.

5. Other headlines

  • The US warned allies in a draft diplomatic letter against joining China's proposed AI governance framework.
  • Nvidia is being described as becoming the 'Federal Reserve of AI'; Goldman Sachs says space is turning into an institutional asset class; carmakers are turning to new motor oil blends amid supply pressure.

Dario Amodei fires back at critics over AI's "crisis of trust"

  • Amodei's first X post in over a month (a two-part reply that reached 10M+ views) rejected investor Gavin Baker's claim that Anthropic's safety warnings backfired, calling the framing "honest" rather than "doom."
  • He rejected the false choice between heavy regulation that favors incumbents and no regulation at all, citing California's SB53 transparency law as proof his asks burden frontier labs like Anthropic more than smaller competitors.
  • Core argument: public distrust stems from decades of corporate overpromising and general institutional distrust, not from safety talk — AI "still hasn't delivered" on its promises.
  • Amodei says the only route back to trust is real breakthroughs in medicine and biology, not marketing campaigns.
  • He denied rumors he envisions Anthropic as "the only private company in the world"; Anthropic researcher Sholto Douglas called the claim "completely false."
  1. The Neuron: Baker compared Amodei's alleged ambition to SBF-level founder delusion (Sam Bankman-Fried, the FTX founder now imprisoned for fraud), and the exchange started on the All-In Podcast.
  2. The Neuron: Douglas argued Anthropic's actual worry is any single company gaining too much power.
  3. Superhuman (Zain Kahn): Amodei argued for a middle path between concentrated and fully distributed AI development, describing his own past statements as balanced.

Covered by: The Rundown AI, The Neuron, Superhuman (Zain Kahn)

1. OpenAI's GPT-5.6-Cyber hacking model goes self-serve on Amazon's marketplace

  • GPT-5.6-Cyber is trained with fewer safety limits so it writes working exploit code, not just bug descriptions.
  • It has already found 400+ privilege-escalation flaws (bugs that let attackers gain higher system permissions) in a single operating system.
  • Access previously required personal vetting by OpenAI; it can now be provisioned like a normal software subscription through an existing company Amazon cloud account.

2. Fine-tuning your own model with Unsloth Studio

  • Fine-tuning (retraining an existing open-source model on your own examples so it picks up new habits) is now point-and-click on a personal computer via Unsloth Studio.
  • Workflow: install, pick a model your hardware can run, build instruction/input/output example triplets, train with QLoRA (adapts a small add-on layer instead of retraining the whole model), then A/B against the original.
  • Quality of examples is the main determinant of results.

Staying Ahead 1

1. Cloudflare's stack for agents that actually execute code

  • Agents SDK runs on Durable Objects (long-lived compute containers that keep each agent's state across restarts) with built-in SQL, WebSockets, routing, scheduling and sessions.
  • Sandboxes (generally available April 2026) give agents isolated Linux environments with a real shell, filesystem and background processes, so no human has to run the output.
  • Sandbox filesystem state persists between calls in a session, enabling clone repo → test → read error → edit → test loops without re-provisioning.
  • Project Think, an experimental preview base class, auto-runs the agentic loop (the iterative decide-act-observe cycle), persists messages and resumes interrupted streams on Durable Object SQLite; long-term recall is handled by a separate Agent Memory service.
  • Agents write state via this.state/this.setState() with automatic persistence; SDK now supports AI SDK v6 and v7 side by side.
  • Channels: chat, WebSockets, email via onEmail plus Cloudflare Email Service (public beta), and voice via @cloudflare/voice beta (withVoiceInput for speech-to-text, withVoice for two-way conversation).
  • Advice: prototype with the Think preview but keep production logic in stable code, and pin the voice package version.
  • Kitesurf, Cloudflare's agent browser shipped August 2026, plus Sandboxes turn agents from code suggesters into ticket-closing automation.
