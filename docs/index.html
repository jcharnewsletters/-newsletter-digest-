CMC Market Pulse

1. EU MiCA Regulatory Implementation Push

  • MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation, the EU's comprehensive crypto rulebook) compliance deadlines require exchanges to implement know-your-customer (KYC, identity verification) checks and transaction monitoring by set dates.
  • Custodial services face new capital and insurance requirements, potentially consolidating the market around larger, better-capitalized providers.
  • EU implementation may set a regulatory precedent other jurisdictions follow or adapt.

2. Bitcoin Volatility Amid Macro Uncertainty

  • Bitcoin volatility remains elevated as traders assess how central bank policy shifts affect digital asset valuations.
  • Regulatory clarity remains elusive in major markets as lawmakers debate how to classify cryptocurrencies, affecting institutional investment decisions.

3. Staking Market Expansion

  • Total value locked (TVL, the dollar value of assets deposited in a protocol) in staking protocols hit new highs, with Ethereum and Solana attracting billions in deposits.
  • Average staking rewards (earnings for locking up crypto to help secure a network) currently range 4-12% annually depending on network and risk profile.
  • Regulatory questions remain over whether staking income counts as passive income or a security-like instrument in different jurisdictions.

4. Layer 2 Adoption Acceleration

  • Arbitrum and Optimism (Layer 2 networks that process transactions off Ethereum's main chain to cut costs) processed record transaction volumes as users migrate from mainnet to reduce gas fees.
  • Transaction costs on Layer 2 solutions are now 10-100x lower than mainnet, driving significant migration from Ethereum's base layer.
  • Developer ecosystem on Layer 2 platforms expanded with major DeFi (decentralized finance) protocols launching multi-chain deployments.

2. Perpetual Futures Market Expansion to Regulated US Venues

  • Perpetual futures (leveraged contracts betting on price moves with no expiration date) generated roughly $90 trillion in annual volume, per Bank of America.
  • Kalshi topped $1 billion in perps volume within one week of launch in June; Coinbase also received CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission) approval.
  • Large Wall Street banks are moving cautiously despite prop trading firms racing in, citing capital requirements, compliance costs, and reputational risk.
  • Use cases include managing weekend risk when traditional futures markets are closed, but thin liquidity and unresolved regulatory classification remain obstacles.

3. Fanatics' Move into Prediction Markets Infrastructure

  • Fanatics acquired Water Street Labs and CX Clearinghouse from BGC Group, gaining a federally regulated exchange and clearinghouse to list and settle its own contracts.
  • This lets Fanatics control prediction market infrastructure independently rather than rely on third-party venues, speeding new market launches.
  • Fanatics Markets has operated in 23 states and four U.S. territories since its late-2024 launch, and plans to combine prediction market data with traditional financial data products.
  • Competition is heating up with Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase, Robinhood, and DraftKings all establishing positions in event-based trading.

CMC Spotlight

1. Tether's XAUt Receives Islamic Finance Certification

  • Tether's gold-backed token (XAUt, a blockchain token representing physical gold holdings) is now certified compliant with Islamic finance principles (Sharia law), which prohibits interest-based instruments and certain commodities.
  • The certification lets Muslim investors and institutions participate in gold tokenization without violating religious financial guidelines.
  • Gold-backed tokens allow fractional ownership and easier transfer of physical gold via blockchain.

2. Implications for Crypto Market Expansion

  • Access to the $2 trillion Islamic finance market represents significant growth potential for blockchain-based asset platforms.
  • The certification signals broader institutional acceptance of tokenized commodities and may prompt other projects to seek similar compliance.
  • Gold tokenization appeals to both traditional finance and crypto participants seeking non-correlated, tangibly-backed assets.

TLDR Crypto

1. Lido's Largest Network Upgrade Since 2023

  • Lido consolidated 8 million stETH ($16.5 billion) onto Ethereum's post-Pectra validator design, its largest upgrade since 2023, cutting total validators by about one-third.
  • The upgrade moves 34 curated node operators to Curated Module v2, requiring them to post locked ETH bonds for financial accountability for the first time in the protocol's five-year history.
  • Migration reduces attestation messages (per-epoch validator confirmation broadcasts, where an epoch is a blockchain time unit) by about 29% but doesn't directly affect gas fees or transaction speed.
  • Annual staking rewards across the protocol will decline by roughly 0.28% due to validator efficiency improvements.

2. SparkKitty Malware Targets Cryptocurrency Holdings

  • SparkKitty is cross-platform malware distributed via the Apple App Store, Google Play, and third-party channels, evolved from 2025's SparkCat malware.
  • It uses OCR (optical character recognition, which converts images to readable text) to scan users' photo libraries for wallet seed phrases (the 12-24 word recovery code granting full access to crypto funds).
  • Two identified apps—iOS app 'coin' and Android app 'SOEX'—were downloaded over 10,000 times before removal from official app stores.

3. Strategic Acquisitions Reshape Crypto Infrastructure

  • Circle acquired IBM's blockchain patent portfolio—680+ patent families and nearly 1,000 issued patents spanning blockchain, banking, and enterprise systems—becoming the leading U.S. blockchain patent holder.
  • Payward (Kraken's parent) acquired Magic Labs' embedded wallet business (60M+ wallets created since 2018) as part of a spree including Bitnomial ($550M), Reap Technologies ($600M), and NinjaTrader ($1.5B).
  • Magic Labs rebranded as Newton Labs to focus on Newton Protocol, an onchain finance authorization layer that entered mainnet beta in June 2026.

4. Ondo Network Separates Execution from Settlement

  • Ondo shifted from its original Ondo Chain plan to the Ondo Network, an execution layer aiming to match centralized exchange speed while keeping settlement non-custodial and onchain.
  • The architecture separates transaction execution from verification and settlement, using secure hardware enclaves (isolated, tamper-resistant processing environments) with a decentralized attestor network to verify approved code.
  • Ondo Perps, the network's first app, is a 24/7 perpetual futures platform for equities and commodities using tokenized RWAs (real-world assets represented as blockchain tokens) as collateral.
  • The ONDO token remains the governance token (grants holders voting rights on protocol decisions) as the network gradually decentralizes from the founding team.

5. Crypto Project Shutdowns in 2026

  • Over 68 crypto projects shut down in 2026, including BitMEX, Polygon zkEVM, and Balancer Labs.
  • Web3 gaming recorded the highest failure density among sectors.
