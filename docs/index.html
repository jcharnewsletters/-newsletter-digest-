Claude in Pharmaceutical Development: Real Lab Results and Real Errors

  • Anthropic acquired Mithril Medicine, a one-person biotech company that used Claude to develop a rare muscle disease treatment in 18 months with 11 outside labs—work typically requiring years and full teams.
  • Anthropic placed Claude in programs at six organizations including Bristol Myers Squibb and Generate Biomedicines, producing measurable gains: immune cells generated roughly 10 times more cancer-spotting receptors in lab-dish tests.
  • At Enveda, Claude reduced manufacturing chemist paperwork from 75% of their time to significantly less, while also catching 7 issues humans missed in QA reviews.
  • Mithril Medicine's Cody Tranbarger used Claude to coordinate contracts, logistics, and experiments across 11 labs for LAMA2-related congenital muscular dystrophy (a rare childhood muscle disease), completing mouse studies in under 18 months.
  • Mithril's own report documented six Claude errors, with at least three reaching outside labs via Claude-drafted emails that passed human editing—showing AI requires expert domain knowledge to catch mistakes before decisions are made.
  • All results remain preclinical (untested in humans); the mouse study was a pilot.

Google's Genie 4: Generative Worlds with Built-In Limitations
