S&P 500 Celebrates Four-Year Bull Market, But Tech Concentration Raises Questions

  • S&P 500 hit a four-year bull market milestone (Oct. 12), up nearly 120% from the October 2022 bear-market low of 3,577, reaching roughly 7,811.
  • Technology now makes up nearly 40% of the index's market value and drove about 12 of the index's 13.6-point return this year, meaning buying the broad index is effectively a concentrated bet on tech.
  • Only two of 11 S&P 500 sectors (tech and energy) are outperforming the index year-to-date; excluding them, the median sector is up just 1% with four sectors negative.
  • Double-digit earnings growth and investors' willingness to pay higher premiums for future earnings have fueled the rally, aided by the AI boom across semiconductors, data centers, power grids, and utilities.
  • Despite Nvidia's market cap ballooning from $144 billion to nearly $6 trillion since the pandemic began, the gap between S&P 500 and tech-sector forward P/E ratios (price-to-earnings multiples based on projected future earnings) has shrunk to pandemic-era lows, suggesting tech may be cheaper than it looks.
  • Market-cap concentration is viewed by some analysts as justified because profits have migrated to a few large tech firms with near-zero marginal costs, letting incremental revenue flow straight to profit.
