Google bundles Gemini AI with Search for K-12 schools, sparking backlash

  • Starting early 2026, K-12 student accounts will have Search, Gemini (Google's AI chatbot), Gemini Notebook, and Gemini in Classroom all on by default unless an administrator changes settings.
  • Search and AI are integrated at the code level, so schools that ban AI will see Search itself show an 'unavailable' message—there's no way to keep Search without AI.
  • Common Sense Media rated Google's AI Search an unacceptable risk for young users in July; Google called that testing narrow but confirmed the AI experience is identical across consumer and school accounts.
  • Major districts (NYC, LA—about 1 million students combined) have already restricted AI this semester; Fairfax County built a plugin just to hide Google's AI summaries from search results.
  • Google's Guided Learning pilot launches this month at select U.S. schools, walking students through homework step-by-step instead of giving answers, with teacher-visible transcripts.
  • Former FTC Chair Lina Khan accused Google of using 'its search monopoly to coerce schools'; Google's public defense only referenced AI-enabled Search, never a Search-without-AI option.
